Knerd — commercial font license
===============================

Knerd is a commercial typeface by Any-Type Foundry. The font files are NOT in this repository — they
were removed before publication and the paths are gitignored. This file records why, and what the
terms are if you drop a licensed copy in locally. They are proprietary. They are NOT covered by the AGPL grant in LICENSE, and no rights to them are passed to
recipients of this repository.

Squirrel Labs Ltd holds a purchased license to use Knerd in Peanut Split and on the official
peanutsplit.com service. That license is between Squirrel Labs Ltd and Any-Type Foundry. It is not
sublicensed here, and it does not authorise anyone else to use, embed, modify, or redistribute these
files.

If you fork Peanut Split, buy your own Knerd license from Any-Type Foundry or replace the display
face.
Substituting is not purely a delete: the CSS side degrades on its own, because the `knerd-outline`
and `knerd-filled` Tailwind families fall through to the sans stack when the @font-face in
apps/web/src/styles/globals.css finds no file. Open Graph image rendering does not degrade —
apps/web/src/server/og/fonts.ts falls back to the bold body face, so share cards render in Roboto
rather than failing — correct, but not the Split look.

Gitignored paths:

    apps/web/public/fonts/knerd-filled.ttf
    apps/web/public/fonts/knerd-filled.woff2
    apps/web/public/fonts/knerd-outline.woff2
    apps/web/src/assets/fonts/knerd-filled.ttf
    apps/web/src/assets/fonts/knerd-outline.ttf
