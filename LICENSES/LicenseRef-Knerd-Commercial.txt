Knerd — commercial font license
===============================

The Knerd font files in this repository are proprietary. They are NOT covered by the AGPL grant in
LICENSE, and no rights to them are passed to recipients of this repository.

Squirrel Labs Ltd holds a purchased license to use Knerd in Peanut Split and on the official
peanutsplit.com service. That license is between Squirrel Labs Ltd and the font's rightsholder. It is
not sublicensed here, and it does not authorise anyone else to use, embed, modify, or redistribute
these files.

If you fork Peanut Split, you must either hold your own Knerd license or replace the display face.
Substituting is not purely a delete: the CSS side degrades on its own, because the `knerd-outline`
and `knerd-filled` Tailwind families fall through to the sans stack when the @font-face in
apps/web/src/styles/globals.css finds no file. Open Graph image rendering does not degrade —
apps/web/src/server/og/fonts.ts reads `knerd-filled.ttf` unguarded, so those routes fail until you
point that loader at a face you are licensed to use.

Affected files:

    apps/web/public/fonts/knerd-filled.ttf
    apps/web/public/fonts/knerd-filled.woff2
    apps/web/public/fonts/knerd-outline.woff2
    apps/web/src/assets/fonts/knerd-filled.ttf
    apps/web/src/assets/fonts/knerd-outline.ttf
