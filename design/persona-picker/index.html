What changed in the concept

Random once, shared everywhere A random character is stored on the member, so every phone sees the same result.
Roles have names “Cozy Ghost” creates more banter than an anonymous icon tile.
The room owns the joke Any link-holder can recast any member, and the change appears live for everyone.
Curated, not uploaded No photos or text means no moderation surface, storage bill, or identity inference.

Thirty personalities, not thirty hairstyles

The catalog is balanced across mischief, cozy, brainy, party and adventure. It covers social energy without mapping any option to a kind of person.
