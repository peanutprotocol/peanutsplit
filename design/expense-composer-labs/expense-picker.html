Codebase scrollbar sweep8 surfaces

The shared drawer is the root. Its nested 80vh + overflow-auto wrapper creates the visible inner bar, clips popovers and gets fragile above a mobile keyboard.

  1. Give every drawer three zones: sticky header, one minmax(0,1fr) scrolling body, sticky action footer.
  2. Use dvh, safe-area padding and one explicit scroll owner. Remove the global touch-move interception.
  3. Portal floating pickers; place down first, collision-flip only when measured space requires it.
  4. Never stack drawers or body locks. Replace settings → notification and scan flows as phases.
  5. Scope overscroll suppression to the room/PWA—not marketing pages.
  6. Standardize page, modal and horizontal-rail primitives; do not globally hide vertical bars.
  7. Keep scan backlog-gated until it is a single modal phase with a proven escape path.
  8. QA 320×568, 390×844 and landscape with keyboard, long groups, focus restore and edge-positioned pickers.
Label-removal rulessemantic

Remove the headings, not the meaning. The selected controls still need to read as a plain-language receipt.

  1. Keep real Amount and Description labels as visually hidden text; a placeholder is not a label.
  2. Say “Asdf2 paid”. A naked avatar can mean payer, participant or current user.
  3. Keep Equally / Exact; those words name a real decision. Lose only the redundant “Split” heading.
  4. Use Today / 29 Jul in the chip while its accessible name remains “Expense date, today”.
  5. Attach errors to the field, focus the first invalid control and announce the Exact remainder as status.
  6. Localize sentence controls as one rich message; never concatenate English fragments in code.
