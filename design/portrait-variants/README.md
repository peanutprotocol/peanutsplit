# Testimonial portrait doodle variants

Review set only. These do not replace the portraits in `apps/web/public/`.

Every variant used:

- the public portrait as the identity reference;
- the Peanut Split reaction strip as the dominant style reference;
- a warm off-white ground, warm dark ink, and limited yellow/lavender/pink accents;
- an explicit ban on polished editorial illustration, realism, smooth vectors,
  gradients, text, logos, emoji, and watermarks.

## Methods

1. **Blind contour** — nearly single-line, very few marks, imperfect joins and
   overshoots.
2. **Ballpoint** — nervous notebook marginalia, repeated contours, scratch-outs
   and uncertain pressure.
3. **Chunky marker** — ten-second felt-tip mascot, blunt shapes, accidental gaps
   and intentionally uneven features.
4. **Rubber stamp** — hand-carved, dry ink, broken edges and slightly
   misregistered features.
5. **Automatic line** — the mildly unhinged option: one escaping continuous
   contour, stray loops and features that almost leave the face.

Konrad's identity anchors were the swept quiff, narrow face, light moustache and
dark high collar. Hugo's were the dark hair swoop, beard, large friendly smile
and pink collar; the cat and strap were explicitly removed.

All ten images were generated with the built-in image generator. Gemini was
requested but was not available as a tool, CLI, credential, or connector in this
workspace, so these are five genuinely different drawing methods rather than a
falsely labeled five-model comparison.
