## Remaining limitation

Git can establish mechanical consistency; it cannot reconstruct who approved
the Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese prose before the merge. The repository
tests casing, dates, links, structure, and prohibited claims, but they are not a
substitute for a fluent editorial review. The commit subject is the only
in-repository record that the localization batch had already been reviewed.

That limitation does not justify a rollback of a coherent, tested descendant.
Future translated releases should name the editorial reviewer or linked review
record in the delivery note when that approval matters.

## Process correction

Substantive product edits should be attributable before histories join:

1. Land integration changes on the feature/integration branch before merging;
   or
2. merge the branches cleanly and put additional product edits in a named
   follow-up commit.

Do not use a merge commit itself as an unlabelled release workspace. If an
exception is unavoidable, its message must list the merge-only areas and the
checks or reviewers that approved them.
