Long description~750 char

Peanut Split turns a group chat into a shared expense room. Paste one link and everyone can open the same room and add what they paid - no account, no install, no app store. It converts across 162 currencies automatically, so a trip billed in three currencies still settles cleanly. Split evenly, by shares, by percentage or by exact amounts. Import an existing group from Splitwise, or scan a receipt to itemise it. Everything works offline and syncs when you reconnect, and it installs to the home screen on iOS, Android and desktop as a Progressive Web App. When it is time to settle up, Peanut Split works out the fewest transfers needed and lets people pay in cash, by bank transfer, or through Peanut. Free, with no ads and no daily limits.
