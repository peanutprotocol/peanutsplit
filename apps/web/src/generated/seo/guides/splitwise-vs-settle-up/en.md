Splitwise Pro is worth considering if you need receipt scanning and expense search. Settle Up
lists offline use and lets you edit exchange rates. Both track shared expenses; the choice depends
on which features your group will use. This comparison uses the companies' documentation, checked
9 September 2026.

## Splitwise vs Settle Up: features to compare

| Feature | Splitwise | Settle Up |
|---|---|---|
| Paid features | Pro includes receipt scanning, itemisation and expense search | Premium includes receipt photos, recurring expenses, Excel export and statistics |
| Currency conversion | Pro converts expenses to the account's default currency | An exchange rate or converted amount can be edited |
| Joining a group | The setup guide starts with an account | Friends can view a balance from a browser link |

Sources: [Splitwise Pro](https://www.splitwise.com/pro),
[Splitwise setup guide](https://kb.splitwise.com/getting-started/how-do-i-use-splitwise),
[Settle Up's developer description](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/settle-up-group-expenses/id737534985)
and [Settle Up tips](https://settleup.io/tips). A feature missing from this table is not necessarily
missing from an app. Check the price and included features before buying a subscription.

## Offline use and exchange rates

Settle Up's [App Store description](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/settle-up-group-expenses/id737534985)
says it works offline and syncs after reconnecting. That is useful to check if your trip will
include places with poor reception.

Settle Up lets you edit the exchange rate or converted amount. Later transactions use the edited
rate until you switch back to the current rate. Its
[official tips](https://settleup.io/tips) explain the setting.

Splitwise's conversion also covers already-settled expenses and can affect other members' balances.
[Its currency guide](https://kb.splitwise.com/balances-and-expenses/how-can-i-manage-a-friendship-or-group-with-multiple-currencies)
describes that behaviour. Our [Splitwise currency explainer](/guides/splitwise-currency-conversion)
covers the steps and rate choice.

## Suggested debts and actual payments

Settle Up allows debt minimisation to be turned off in group settings. With it on, the suggested
payee can differ from the person who paid for the original expense.
[Source: Settle Up tips](https://settleup.io/tips).
[This worked example](/guides/why-do-i-owe-someone-i-never-paid) explains the calculation.

Recording a payment and sending money are different actions. Splitwise supports recording payments
made elsewhere and offers payment methods such as Splitwise Pay and Pay by Bank in supported
countries. Check the options available to your group in the
[Splitwise setup guide](https://kb.splitwise.com/getting-started/how-do-i-use-splitwise).

## Moving from Splitwise to Settle Up

Settle Up documents an import of group members and their current balances. Export from the Splitwise
web app, then import while creating a Settle Up group. The
[instructions](https://settleup.io/tips) do not promise a full expense-history import. Keep the
original export and check what arrived before relying on the new group.

If your current app already meets the group's needs, keeping it avoids asking everyone to move.

## When a browser room is enough

Peanut Split is an option for a group that wants to add expenses through a shared link. Anyone with
the link can open the room and add to it. There is no account or required app-store install; adding
Split to a device is optional. Save the link, because there is no login or password recovery.

Pay by your chosen method outside Split, then record it in the room. Split does not send money or
verify payments with a bank. If you need to request payment, these
[message examples](/guides/ask-a-friend-to-pay-you-back) give you a starting point.

Split is free to use and has no paid tier.
