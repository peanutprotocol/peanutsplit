Two people in the group chat have already named a different app, and the receipts keep arriving while
the decision sits there; the trip ends with no app at all, and the deposit never comes back to
whoever paid it. That person says nothing about it for a year. Every statement about Splitwise or
Settle Up below is a sentence its own company publishes, with the page and the date under it.

## Splitwise vs Settle Up: what both of them do

Both apps do the same central job, and both describe it in nearly the same words.

> Keep track of shared expenses, balances, and who owes who.

*[splitwise.com](https://www.splitwise.com/), checked 12 August 2026*

> Settle Up keeps track of your gang's expenses – great for travellers, flatmates, couples and others.

*[settleup.io](https://settleup.io/), checked 12 August 2026*

The arithmetic is not where they differ. They differ in what each one asks of the people who did not
pick it.

## What each one puts behind a paid tier

Splitwise prints the contents of its paid tier on its own Pro page: "Receipt scanning", "Charts and
graphs", "Expense search", "Transaction import" and "Itemization".

*[splitwise.com/pro](https://www.splitwise.com/pro), checked 12 August 2026*

"Unlimited expenses" is on the same list, which is how a reader finds out that the free path is
capped at all.

Settle Up sells a tier of its own, and prints its contents on the App Store listing: "Receipt
photos", "Recurring & future expenses", "Custom categories", "Export to Excel" and "Statistics".

> Statistics: View charts on spend by categories and members.

*[Settle Up on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/settle-up-group-expenses/id737534985), checked 12 August 2026*

Both lists sell the receipt and the chart. The Splitwise list carries two things the Settle Up list
does not: reading the receipt rather than storing the photograph, and a search. For a household three
years into recording expenses, a search box is the difference between finding the boiler repair and
scrolling for it.

## What Settle Up does well

Settle Up works without a connection, and says so on that same listing.

> No internet? No problem — it works offline and syncs when you're back.

*[Settle Up on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/settle-up-group-expenses/id737534985), checked 12 August 2026*

That is a real capability and nothing else in this comparison replaces it. The expense goes in where
you are standing and reconciles when the phone finds a signal.

Settle Up also lets a group hold its own exchange rate steady.

> You can edit the exchange rate or the calculated amount in group currency. If you do that, all future transactions will use this exchange rate until you change back to current by clicking on three dots on the card. This is useful when you want to use one exchange rate for the whole vacation.

*[settleup.io/tips](https://settleup.io/tips), checked 12 August 2026*

A rate the whole group agreed on is one fewer argument when the card statements land.
[Splitwise converts on a button](/guides/splitwise-currency-conversion) instead, and its help centre
article on multiple currencies says how far one press reaches.

> The conversion applies to all expenses in the group or friendship, including ones that are already settled. This can affect balances for other people who share those expenses.

*[Splitwise help centre](https://kb.splitwise.com/balances-and-expenses/how-can-i-manage-a-friendship-or-group-with-multiple-currencies), checked 17 August 2026*

Settle Up's rate governs the expenses still to come. Splitwise's press reaches back over the ones the
group had finished with.

And it lets a group switch off the netting,
[the arithmetic that reassigns the payee](/guides/why-do-i-owe-someone-i-never-paid).

> It minimizes the number of transactions between people by transferring debts, so you might end up paying to somebody you don't directly owe. If you don't like that, you can turn off debt minimization in the Edit group screen.

*[settleup.io/tips](https://settleup.io/tips), checked 12 August 2026*

Turning it off trades a shorter payment list for one where everybody pays the person they actually
owe. That switch and the rate field are both the group's to set rather than Settle Up's.

## Both of them need an account

Splitwise opens with one and does not treat it as optional.

> Sign up for an account. You can sign up from the mobile app or at www.splitwise.com/signup.

*[Splitwise help centre](https://kb.splitwise.com/getting-started/how-do-i-use-splitwise), checked 12 August 2026*

> We think it's important that everyone in a Splitwise group can log in and see their balance.

*[Splitwise help centre](https://kb.splitwise.com/getting-started/can-i-add-a-friend-without-adding-their-email-address-or-phone-number), checked 12 August 2026*

Settle Up puts the same requirement in its privacy policy, which lists identifying your account among
the reasons it holds an email address.

> to identify users who have access to your group, to identify your account, to back up your data, to generate an email with data export from the app, for marketing campaigns (which you can opt out of)

*[settleup.io/privacy_policy](https://settleup.io/privacy_policy), checked 12 August 2026*

Each has a partial way round it, and neither is a way out. Splitwise's placeholder is "someone
tracked by name, without contact info", which is a name in your list rather than somebody who can log
in. Settle Up's is a view-only link.

> Share a group link so friends can view the balance in their browser without downloading anything.

*[Settle Up on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/settle-up-group-expenses/id737534985), checked 12 August 2026*

Reading the balance is not the same as adding the taxi to it.

## Both of them record the payment rather than move it

Splitwise names both halves of this in one sentence.

> You can record a payment made outside Splitwise (cash, a bank transfer, etc.), or in supported countries, send money directly through Splitwise using a payment method such as Splitwise Pay, Pay by Bank, or our third-party integrations with Venmo and PayPal.

*[Splitwise help centre](https://kb.splitwise.com/getting-started/how-do-i-use-splitwise), checked 12 August 2026*

Outside those supported countries the entry is a record of something that happened elsewhere. Inside
them, the payment still leaves the app.

> When you select one of these options, you will be redirected to PayPal or Venmo to complete the payment.

*[Splitwise help centre](https://kb.splitwise.com/payment-integrations/how-do-i-send-money-via-paypal-or-venmo), checked 12 August 2026*

Settle Up's Lightning page covers paying over the Bitcoin Lightning network, and states its own
position on that money without hedging it.

> No extra fees (other than the network fees), no involvement of Settle Up, you pay directly the person you owe.

*[settleup.io/lightning](https://settleup.io/lightning), checked 12 August 2026*

Neither app is where the money is. Both are where the group agrees what the money was. Getting it to
move is still [the message that asks for it back](/guides/ask-a-friend-to-pay-you-back).

## Neither company prints a price on the page that sells the tier

Splitwise's Pro page lists what the tier adds and prints no figure. Settle Up prints no figure on
settleup.io either. The software directories that rank for this comparison do print numbers. Those
numbers disagree with one another, and not one of them says where its figure came from. Read the
price inside whichever app you are about to pay, on the day you pay it.

## Switching between them: what moves across, what stays

Settle Up publishes the steps for arriving from Splitwise.

> You can easily import the current members and their balances into Settle Up. Just export your data in the group settings of the Splitwise web app and import them while creating your group in Settle Up.

*[settleup.io/tips](https://settleup.io/tips), checked 12 August 2026*

### What moves across

- The current members of the group.
- The balance each of them is carrying on the day you export.

### What stays

- The expenses behind those balances: who paid for what, and when.
- Anything attached to an expense, since the import names members and balances only.
- Any other group, unless you export each one separately.

The move is an export on a laptop, a new group on the other side, and a message asking everybody to
install a second thing. Enough of the export screens!

## When Splitwise or Settle Up is the better tool

Settle Up is the better tool for a group that spends real time without a signal, and for a trip that
wants one agreed rate across the whole week rather than a different one every day.

Splitwise is the better tool for a household with years of history to search, and for a group that
photographs receipts and wants them read.

Either of them beats a third app if your group has already installed one and already agreed on it. A
migration is paid for by the people who did not ask for it.
