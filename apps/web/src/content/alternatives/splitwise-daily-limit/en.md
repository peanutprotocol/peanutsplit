## The published limit

<Quote source="feedback.splitwise.com">
Splitwise has introduced a daily expense limit for our free tier.
</Quote>

The Pro help page gives the number:

<Quote source="kb.splitwise.com">
Unlimited expenses: Add as many expenses as you need without hitting a limit (free users can add up to 4 expenses each day).
</Quote>

These pages were checked on 31 July 2026. They did not specify an exact reset time.

## Options if you reach the limit

Splitwise's help page suggests waiting until the limit resets or asking a friend to add the expense on your behalf. Make sure the expense still names the correct payer.

It also says Pro removes the daily cap. The annual plans described on that page include a Trip Pass that gives one chosen trip group Pro features for 30 days. Check whether someone in your group has an available pass.

## What Pro costs

Check the price shown to your account before subscribing. Splitwise's terms explain why prices can vary:

<Quote source="splitwise.com/terms">
The price at which Splitwise Pro is offered may vary due to date of subscription, country, local taxes and regulations, promotions, and Splitwise reserves the right to change the price and subscription benefits at its sole discretion.
</Quote>

## Moving to Peanut Split

Split is free to use and has no daily expense cap. You can bring an existing group through its CSV export.

<Steps title="Import your group">
<Step title="Export from Splitwise">Open the group settings on splitwise.com and choose “Export as spreadsheet”.</Step>
<Step title="Review the import">Open the import page, select the CSV and review the warnings, room currency and balances.</Step>
<Step title="Share the new link">Keep the original group until everyone agrees the balances match. Then use the new room for further expenses.</Step>
</Steps>

[Open the import page](/import)

Receipt photos do not transfer. A source file can contain up to twenty people. Older entries may be combined into “Balance brought forward” rows to fit the room's 500 expense entries.

## When staying on Splitwise makes sense

If the limit rarely affects your group, waiting for the reset may be easier than switching. Check the features and history you want to keep before moving.

Split records payments but does not make or verify them. Pay each other using an agreed method, then mark the payment in the room.
