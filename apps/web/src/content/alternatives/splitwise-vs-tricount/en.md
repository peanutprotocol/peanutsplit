## Expense limits and cost

Splitwise's help centre describes its free daily limit:

<Quote source="kb.splitwise.com">
Add as many expenses as you need without hitting a limit (free users can add up to 4 expenses each day).
</Quote>

If you reach it, Splitwise suggests waiting for the reset or asking a friend to add an expense on your behalf.

Tricount's comparison page describes its service as “No ads, no limits”. Its homepage says:

<Quote source="tricount.com">
Experience the easiest way to track group expenses, 100% free!
</Quote>

For a group entering many expenses at once, the daily cap is a useful difference to check.

## Currency conversion

Splitwise supports expenses in different currencies, but its help centre limits conversion to Pro:

<Quote source="kb.splitwise.com">
Only Pro subscribers can convert, but all group members will see expenses in their converted form.
</Quote>

It also explains the boundary of Simplify Debts:

<Quote source="kb.splitwise.com">
If your group uses more than one currency, Simplify Debts works within each currency separately. It does not combine or convert balances across currencies.
</Quote>

Tricount describes automatic conversion on its homepage:

<Quote source="tricount.com">
tricount automatically converts expenses into a single currency for easy tracking.
</Quote>

If you want several currencies converted into one balance without a subscription, Tricount's published features fit that requirement.

## Accounts and access

Splitwise offers iPhone, Android and web access. Its help centre says:

<Quote source="kb.splitwise.com">
We think it's important that everyone in a Splitwise group can log in and see their balance.
</Quote>

You can add a placeholder friend by name in the web version. Tricount advertises “No Registration” and shares groups by link:

<Quote source="tricount.com">
Share your tricount link with your friends. Everyone can add their own expenses, see what's already been added.
</Quote>

## Payments

Splitwise describes recording a payment this way:

<Quote source="splitwise.com">
Settle up with a friend and record any cash or online payment.
</Quote>

Tricount advertises bank payment requests and automatic expense entry with a bunq card:

<Quote source="tricount.com">
Send a payment request straight from the app and get paid directly to your bank account.
</Quote>

<Quote source="tricount.com">
Pay with your bunq card and the expense gets automatically added to your tricount.
</Quote>

Check the availability of Tricount's bank and card features in your country.

## Where Peanut Split fits

Split runs in the browser and lets everyone add expenses through a room link without creating accounts. It is free to use, has no paid tier and converts 156 currencies at the day's indicative rate.

New expenses can queue on your device without a connection. Editing expenses and recording payments need a connection. Split suggests who should pay whom, but you make the payment separately and record it yourself. It does not verify with a bank.

## Choosing for your group

Tricount is worth considering for its advertised free conversion and lack of an expense cap. Splitwise may suit a group that already uses its accounts and history or wants its Pro features. Split is an option if browser access without accounts is the main requirement.
