## Group access and personal details

Settle Up's homepage describes its expense tracking:

<Quote source="settleup.io">
Settle Up keeps track of your gang's expenses – great for travellers, flatmates, couples and others.
</Quote>

Its privacy policy says group members can access the following information:

<Quote source="settleup.io/privacy_policy">
Other users who are in the same group(s) as you can access your: Name, Email, Profile picture(s), Transaction & other data you enter into the app
</Quote>

In Split, members enter a display name. Anyone with the room link can access the room, so share it only with the group.

Settle Up also advertises browser access for viewing balances:

<Quote source="Settle Up on the App Store">
No app required: Share a group link so friends can view the balance in their browser without downloading anything.
</Quote>

Split's room link lets people add expenses as well as view balances without creating an account.

## Offline use

Settle Up's App Store listing says:

<Quote source="Settle Up on the App Store">
No internet? No problem — it works offline and syncs when you're back.
</Quote>

In Split, opening a room for the first time needs a connection. After that, new expenses can wait on your device and send when you reconnect. Editing expenses and recording payments need a connection. If broader offline use is important, check Settle Up's features against the tasks your group needs.

## Moving an existing group

Split does not import Settle Up files directly. You can re-enter outstanding expenses and their shares manually, then check that the balances match. Keep the original group for its history.

Starting at the end of a trip or billing period avoids maintaining two overlapping sets of expenses.

## Payment plans

Settle Up explains why a suggested payment might go to someone you did not originally owe:

<Quote source="settleup.io/tips">
It minimizes the number of transactions between people by transferring debts, so you might end up paying to somebody you don't directly owe.
</Quote>

Split also combines debts into a short payment plan. Pay each other by an agreed method, then record the payment. Split does not move money or verify it with a bank.

## When Settle Up may suit you better

If your group already uses Settle Up and wants to keep its history and account-based access, there may be no reason to move. Split is useful when everyone wants to enter expenses in a browser without signing up.

Split is free to use and has no paid tier. It converts expenses in 156 currencies at the day's indicative rate.
