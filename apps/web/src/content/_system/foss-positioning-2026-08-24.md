# FOSS positioning ruling — 2026-08-24

## Product-owner direction

- Squirrel Labs is the correct entity.
- Squirrel Labs is currently the sole maintainer and pays every project cost, including maintainer
  work hours and operation of the official service.
- The fair deal permits a few non-intrusive Peanut references on the official host. They are never
  spammy, forced, preselected, nagging, or feature-gating, and they are not a downstream license
  condition.
- The project is not optimizing for external contributors.
- FOSS may broaden the existing “free” acquisition surface, but must not be used as a synonym for
  hosted zero-price.

## Page ruling

Expand the existing canonical `/splitwise-alternative`; do not add parallel `foss`, `oss`, `libre`,
or `open-source-splitwise-alternative` doorway pages. That page already owns the commercial query,
has dated first-party competitor evidence, and has three real locale siblings. A second route would
compete for the same intent and repeat the same comparison.

The substantive addition must:

1. separate official-host price from software-license freedom;
2. link an exact source/license/documentation receipt;
3. name what can be self-hosted and the operator's responsibilities and limits;
4. state Squirrel Labs' role and the bounded Peanut-reference policy;
5. preserve the existing “when Splitwise is better” concession;
6. publish only after the public-source, rights, security, custom-origin, and immutable-release gates.

Use `FOSS` naturally once in the English rendered page. Do not seed acronym variants across guides,
footer anchors, or additional URLs.

## Killed ideas

- FOSS as merely another spelling of “free.” It conflates price and rights and reads as openwashing.
- Multiple exact-match pages. They are near-duplicate acquisition doors, not distinct answers.
- Contributor-growth theatre. A standard license does not require a foundation, roadmap election,
  “good first issue” funnel, response SLA, or solicited feature pull requests.
- A Peanut promotion rider. Promotion is an official-host editorial choice, never a software right
  or license condition.
- Publishing the SEO claim before the repository and immutable source receipt exist. A branch-only
  candidate is allowed; an indexed unsupported claim is not.
