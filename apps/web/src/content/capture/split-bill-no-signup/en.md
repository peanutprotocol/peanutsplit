# Split a bill with no sign-up or download

Peanut Split lets you share expenses through a web link. Create a room, send the link and enter a name. Everyone can add what they paid without an email address, password or download.

<Steps title="Join and add an expense">
<Step title="Open the room link">It opens in your phone or computer browser.</Step>
<Step title="Enter your name">Choose the name the group will see next to your expenses.</Step>
<Step title="Add what you paid">Enter the amount and select the people sharing the cost. The room updates the balances. <Cast name="ana" size="sm" /></Step>
</Steps>

## Keep the link

Anyone with the room link can access the room. Save it in the group chat and share it only with the people taking part. There is no login or password recovery if everyone loses the link.

## Good to know

Split is free to use and has no paid tier.

Pay each other by cash, bank transfer or another method you agree on, then record the payment in Split. Split does not move money or check with your bank.
