# How to split Bali trip costs with friends

Agree each person's share of the villa before booking, then record shared transport, food and other costs as you pay them. Include cash payments as well as card purchases so the final balance covers both.

<Steps title="Track the shared costs">
<Step title="Choose the room currency">Rupiah is useful when most expenses are priced locally. You can still add a booking paid in another supported currency.</Step>
<Step title="Record accommodation and deposits separately">Keep a refundable deposit identifiable so you can account for it when it is returned.</Step>
<Step title="Add cash purchases promptly">Record the driver, meals and laundry under whoever paid. Select only the people sharing each expense. <Cast name="mo" size="sm" /></Step>
</Steps>

## If someone stays for fewer nights

Agree whether the accommodation is split by booked places or by nights stayed. An early departure does not reduce the villa's booking price, so decide who covers that cost before someone leaves. Check their balance before they go and agree how any later refund will be shared.
