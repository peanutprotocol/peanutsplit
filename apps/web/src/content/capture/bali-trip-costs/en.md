# What a Bali trip costs, and who owes what

Split the villa by the month and by the bed, and put everything paid in cash into the same place as everything paid by card. The second half is the one groups get wrong.

Bali is the trip where the money leaves no trace. The villa is a bank transfer from one person, the driver is a folded note, the scooters are four separate rentals at four different prices, and the big shop is whoever was in the car. Two weeks in, the group has spent a great deal of money and has no idea how it landed.

A room fixes the record rather than the spending. The link goes in the group chat on day one, everybody types what they paid as they pay it, and an expense added with no signal waits on the phone until the wifi comes back.

## When a bank transfer still wins

Two people sharing one villa fifty-fifty for a month have one number between them, and a message settles it. A room earns its keep in the house of five where the villa was a transfer, the driver was cash and somebody is leaving on the 14th.
