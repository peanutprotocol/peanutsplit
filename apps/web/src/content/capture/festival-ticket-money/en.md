# Getting festival ticket money back without asking twice

Put the tickets in a room the afternoon they clear, at what they cost, and send the link. The debt then belongs to a page rather than to whoever is willing to bring it up.

Somebody sat in a queue in October with six tabs open and came out with eight tickets on one card. Nine months later, four of those people have paid, two have paid part of it, and the person who bought them has stopped mentioning it, because it has been long enough that mentioning it now would be strange.

The amount is rarely the problem. It is that the asking falls on the person who already did the favour, and asking twice costs more socially than the ticket is worth. A room does the asking by existing.

## When a payment request still wins

One ticket bought for one friend is a single transfer and needs nothing else. A room is for the camp of eight where the tickets, the van and the shop were paid by three different people at three different times, and nobody can hold the shape of it in their head.

## Good to know

The official service is free to use and has no paid tier. A room holds up to twenty people.

The room nets the weekend down, so the camp makes two or three transfers rather than twenty. Split will not chase anybody, so there are no reminders and no nudges into the group chat. Split records a payment rather than making one.
