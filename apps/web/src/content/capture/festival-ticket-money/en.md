# Festival ticket money and repayments

After buying festival tickets for friends, send each person the amount they owe, including their share of the booking fee. Agree a payment date and record repayments as they arrive. A shared Split room lets everyone check the amounts.

<Steps title="Record tickets and repayments">
<Step title="Add the ticket purchase">Enter the total under the person who paid. Use individual shares if the tickets cost different amounts.</Step>
<Step title="Share the room link">The group can see the cost and their balances without creating accounts. <Cast name="bea" size="sm" /></Step>
<Step title="Record payments and later expenses">Mark repayments only after they happen. Add shared transport, food and camping costs separately.</Step>
</Steps>

## If someone sells their ticket

Check who receives the resale money. If the buyer pays the original ticket holder, that does not repay the person who booked. Record a repayment only when money reaches the person owed, and agree how any resale loss or fee will be covered.

## Good to know

Peanut Split is free to use and has no paid tier.

Pay each other by cash, bank transfer or another method you agree on, then record the payment in Split. Split does not move money or check with your bank.
