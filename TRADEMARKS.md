# Trademark and naming policy

The licenses in this repository cover copyright. They grant no rights to trademarks. "Peanut",
"Peanut Split", "Split", "Squirrel Labs", the logo, and the mascots identify who makes and runs this
software, and remain Squirrel Labs Ltd's.

The artwork is a deliberate split: the mascots, logo, background and hand illustration ship under
`CC-BY-4.0`, so you may copy and modify the files. Trademark law still governs using them as a
_source identifier_. You can redraw the mascot into your fork; you cannot use it to suggest your fork
is Peanut.

## What you may do

- Run, modify, and redistribute the software, including commercially, under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
- Say truthfully what your version is based on: "a fork of Peanut Split", "compatible with Peanut
  Split". Nominative use of a name to identify the thing you are talking about needs no permission.
- Copy and adapt the `CC-BY-4.0` artwork with attribution.
- Remove every official-host Peanut reference from your build. Nothing in the license requires you to
  keep a promotional link, badge, logo, or referral parameter, and nothing phones home.

## What you may not do

- Name your fork, service, or app "Peanut Split", or a name close enough to be confused with it.
- Use the marks or the logo as your own branding, or in a way that suggests Squirrel Labs Ltd
  sponsors, endorses, operates, or supports your version.
- Register the marks, or a confusingly similar mark, anywhere.

## Fork naming

Pick your own name. If you want to state the lineage, put it after your name rather than inside it —
"Ledgerly, based on Peanut Split" reads as a fact; "Peanut Split Pro" reads as us.

These rules apply equally to everyone. They do not restrict use, modification, or redistribution of
the licensed code, and they will not be used as a back door to require promotional links or
phone-home behaviour.

## Third-party marks

Competitor names on the comparison and alternatives pages belong to their owners and are used to
identify the products being compared. No affiliation or endorsement is implied, in either direction.

Questions about naming or the marks: open an issue on the repository.
