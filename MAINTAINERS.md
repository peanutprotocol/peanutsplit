# Maintainers

**Squirrel Labs** is the sole current upstream maintainer of Peanut Split.

Squirrel Labs decides the upstream roadmap, supported releases, security fixes, repository settings,
and whether a proposed change is reviewed or merged. Maintenance status may change later, but there
is no implied community governance, succession promise, or support service.

For interaction expectations, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). For sensitive reports, see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
